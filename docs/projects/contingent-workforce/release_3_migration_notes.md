# Release 3: what changes in the published workbooks

Arcadia Systems is fictional and all data is synthetic.

Release 3 changes the data under Dashboards #1 and #2 in two steps. This page lists, phase by phase, every field, calc and value a workbook will notice, so the republish (phase 10) is a checklist rather than a hunt. The build books in `docs/projects/headcount-fte-walk/` and `docs/projects/compensation-walk/` describe the dashboards **as published on release 2** and are left as they are until phase 10.

## Phase 1: part-time removed (2026-10-14)

Every employee is 1.0 FTE. People, hires, exits, moves, promotions, seats and requisitions are **identical to release 2** (the generator keeps every random draw; only FTE is forced to 1.0). What changes is FTE and cost.

## Fix carried in release 3: Growth by Region follows the group (logged 2026-10-14)

**What is wrong on the published dashboard.** With a group selected (for example Function = Commercial, FY26), the KPI cards show that group, but **Growth by Region** still shows the whole company ("Arcadia overall +5.6% (28,436 | 30,015)"). The two cards on the same page tell different stories.

**Why.** The sheet `Region Headcount` was built on 2026-10-10 from the walk data source's Country-view rows. That source has no region-by-group breakdown, so the build guide deliberately made it follow Period only and said "do not add `p_View` or `p_Group` filters" ([map guide, section 4](../compensation-walk/tableau_office_moves_map_guide.md)). The data to fix it already exists: the **region walk** query (`tableau/custom_sql_region_walk.sql`, mart `mart_region_walk`) is keyed by period × view × group × region, and per group its regions add up exactly to the walk's opening and closing (test 57). The published sheet was never moved onto it.

**The fix (in the phase 10 republish of Dashboard #2).**

1. Add the region walk as a data source, if the workbook does not have it yet (same connection, new Custom SQL).
2. Rebuild `Region Headcount` on it, filtered like the walk: `from_month_end = p_From`, `to_month_end = p_To`, `view_name = p_View`, `group_id = p_Group`. Open HC = `SUM(opening_headcount)`, Close HC = `SUM(closing_headcount)`, by `region`.
3. Retarget the subtitle's totals to the selected group (`{ FIXED : SUM(...) }` over the same filtered rows, with the filters added to context) and rename "Arcadia overall" to the group's name, so the subtitle reads "Commercial +4.8% (7,441 | 7,795)".
4. Remove the note "do not add `p_View` or `p_Group` filters" from the guide.

**Check after the fix** (FY26, Jun 30 2025 to Jun 30 2026, warehouse as of release 3):

| Selection | Overall | Regions (opening \| closing, growth) |
|---|---|---|
| Company | 28,438 \| 30,005, +5.5% | Latin America 2,225 \| 2,356 +5.9%; Europe 6,665 \| 7,043 +5.7%; Asia Pacific 8,524 \| 9,005 +5.6%; Middle East & Africa 1,115 \| 1,175 +5.4%; North America 9,909 \| 10,426 +5.2% |
| Function = Commercial | 7,441 \| 7,795, +4.8% | Asia Pacific 1,951 \| 2,071 +6.2%; Europe 1,854 \| 1,957 +5.6%; North America 2,461 \| 2,553 +3.7%; Latin America 755 \| 781 +3.4%; Middle East & Africa 420 \| 433 +3.1% |

In each case the overall numbers must equal the People card's opening and closing for the same selection. Note the published workbook also runs on an older extract (it shows 28,436 \| 30,015 for the company, the warehouse now has 28,438 \| 30,005); the republish refreshes both cards together.
