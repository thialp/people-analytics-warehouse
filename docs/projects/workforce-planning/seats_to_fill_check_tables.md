# Seats to Fill: check tables (phase 5)

Every number the page shows, for the selections the build is tested on, computed from the forecast marts the way the page computes them. Report date Jun 30, 2026; forward year FY27. Generated by [`forecast/check_tables.py`](../../../forecast/check_tables.py); rerun it after a refresh. Arcadia Systems is fictional; all data is synthetic.

**Definitions.** Central numbers are the average of the 100 draws (they add up, so the bridge and the functions reconcile). Ranges are P10 to P90 across the draws, summed over the selection per draw. Attainment is forecast fills over the forward Budget's planned fills. Time to fill is Kaplan–Meier over PIDs approved in the last four quarters; the median wait is interpolated between the ten-day boundaries around 50%. The backtest range for a selection is Σ mean ± 1.2816 × √(Σ variance + (shared cv × Σ mean)²).
